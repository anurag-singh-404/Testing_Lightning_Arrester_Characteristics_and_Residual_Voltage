### About the Experiment 
Aim:-
To study the characteristics of a lightning arrester and measure its residual voltage.
Experiment-
A lightning arrester protects electrical equipment from high-voltage surges caused by lightning or switching. In this experiment, a surge voltage is applied to the arrester, and its response is observed. The residual voltage across the arrester is measured during the surge.

Working Principle-
Under normal voltage, the arrester offers high resistance. During a surge, it conducts current and diverts it to earth, limiting the voltage across the protected equipment.

Procedure-
Connect the lightning arrester to the test circuit.
Apply the specified surge voltage.
Observe the arrester's current and voltage.
Measure the residual voltage during the surge.
Compare the results with the arrester's rated values.
Result-
The lightning arrester's voltage-current characteristics and residual voltage are studied to evaluate its ability to protect electrical equipment from voltage surges.
Conclusion-
A lightning arrester limits dangerous overvoltages and protects electrical equipment by safely diverting surge current to earth.
